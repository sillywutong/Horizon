# Advertising practical guides profile

Use this profile for practical, how-to guides and lectures from low-volume, trusted marketing education sources (such as Moz and Neil Patel). Typical items explain how to use AI in advertising and marketing, how advertisers and publishers are using AI tools, and step-by-step methods for planning, running, measuring, and improving campaigns, including search and content marketing.

This profile is assigned explicitly to selected sources. Do not use it for news reports, funding or personnel news, or event promotion.
