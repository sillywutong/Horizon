# Reader

The reader is an engineer at a programmatic advertising platform who wants practical, hands-on knowledge of how advertisers, marketers, and publishers actually do their work, especially how they use AI tools. This profile is used only for a few trusted, low-volume sources, and the reader wants to keep nearly everything they publish.

# Evaluation goal

Rank the item relative to other practical guides. Every item from these sources should score at least 7; use the score only to order items, not to exclude them.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Essential.** A concrete, step-by-step guide or lecture on using AI in advertising or marketing, or on how advertisers or publishers apply AI tools, with specific workflows, prompts, tools, examples, or measured results.
- **8: High value.** A practical how-to on campaign planning, execution, measurement, or optimization (including search and content marketing), with specific steps and examples.
- **7: Useful.** A practical guide that is more general, introductory, or opinion-led, or any other item from these sources. Do not score below 7, except score 0-2 for pure event promotion, product sales pages, or recruiting posts.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward specificity, actionable steps, real examples, and honest discussion of limitations. Distinguish measured results from vendor claims.

Use three to five specific topic tags, such as the tool or channel and the task (for example `AI创意生成`, `Google Ads`, `内容营销`, `AI搜索`).
