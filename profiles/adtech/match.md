# Advertising technology profile

Use this profile for content about how digital advertising is bought, sold, priced, targeted, optimized, and measured, written from the perspective of any party in the advertising ecosystem: ad platforms and ad tech companies, advertisers and brand marketers, agencies, publishers, data providers, measurement vendors, and users.

Typical items include:

- New or changed ad products, bidding and optimization logic, auction mechanics, targeting, identity, or measurement features from major or fast-rising ad platforms (for example Meta, Google, Amazon, Netflix, Walmart, AppLovin, TikTok, The Trade Desk, Microsoft, Apple, OpenAI ads, retail media networks, CTV platforms).
- Programmatic infrastructure: DSPs, SSPs, exchanges, header bidding, supply path optimization, deal types, ad serving, clean rooms, signals and identity after cookie and device-ID loss.
- Measurement and attribution: last-touch and multi-touch attribution, incrementality and lift testing, conversion lift optimization, MMM, geo experiments, conversion APIs, and debates about which method fits which advertiser, channel, or funnel stage.
- AI applied directly inside advertising: AI-driven bidding, creative generation, campaign automation, agentic media buying, and AI-native ad formats.
- Analytical essays, surveys, and debates that explain why an ad tech mechanism works, where it fails, and who benefits.
- The advertiser and brand-marketing perspective: campaign case studies, go-to-market playbooks, community, creator, and influencer marketing, campaign planning and performance tuning, and real-world use of agentic or AI advertising tools.
- Research, surveys, and analysis of what advertisers, agencies, publishers, data providers (including DMPs and clean rooms), third-party measurement vendors, or users think, need, and struggle with: trending opinions, pain points, budget shifts, and new findings about their behavior.

Do not use this profile for:

- SEO, organic search ranking, or generative-engine optimization.
- Funding rounds, valuations, M&A, earnings recaps without product or mechanism detail, executive hires and departures, lawsuits framed as corporate drama, scandals, or gossip.
- Event, conference, webinar, or awards announcements and promotions.
- Commentary on how AI affects society, jobs, or industries in general when it does not describe AI being used inside advertising or marketing systems.
