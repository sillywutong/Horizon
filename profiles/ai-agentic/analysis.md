# Reader

The reader is an algorithm engineer who uses LLMs and agents at work and wants to understand them better. They only want a small amount of this content each day: clear explanations of how things work, practical how-to guidance, and the truly important frontier-model releases. Be strict.

# Evaluation goal

Score whether the item explains a mechanism, gives practical guidance the reader can apply, or covers an important release from a major frontier lab. Everything else should score low.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Essential.** A clear, well-reasoned explanation of why or how an LLM, agentic workflow, or robotics system works, with concrete examples or evidence; or a detailed, practical guide to using LLMs or agents in real work with specific workflows, prompts, tools, and lessons learned.
- **7-8: High value.** A solid explanation or how-to that is narrower or less detailed; or a new flagship model or major feature release from a major lab, or substantive analysis and credible user feedback about such a release. Major labs are Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google (Gemini), Meta, and leading Chinese teams: DeepSeek, Alibaba (Qwen), Moonshot AI (Kimi), Zhipu (GLM), ByteDance (Doubao / Seed), MiniMax, and Tencent (Hunyuan). Score Chinese labs' releases by the same standard as the others.
- **5-6: Marginal.** A brief note on a major-lab release that adds little beyond the announcement; a minor variant or point update from a major lab; or an explanation or guide that is shallow or mostly repeats known material.
- **3-4: Low value.** Releases or benchmarks of small, niche, or fine-tuned models from other labs, general industry analysis, opinion without mechanism or practical detail.
- **0-2: Off topic or noise.** Funding, business strategy, personnel, policy, event or podcast promotion, link roundups, general commentary on AI's impact on society or jobs.

# Evaluation guidance

Titles starting with "Why", "How", "How to", or ending in "explained" are strong signals, but confirm with the body that the item delivers an explanation or actionable guidance. Podcast episode pages score by the substance of their written content, not the guest's reputation. Distinguish lab claims and benchmarks from independent evaluation and real user experience.

Use three to five specific topic tags, such as the model or tool and the topic (for example `Claude`, `GPT`, `agent workflow`, `reasoning`, `prompting`).
