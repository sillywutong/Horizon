# LLM and agents profile (strict)

Use this profile for content about large language models and AI agents that does one of three things:

1. Explains how something works: the mechanisms behind LLMs, reasoning, training, agentic workflows, tool use, or robotics (typical titles: "Why ...", "How ... works", "... explained").
2. Gives practical guidance on using LLMs or agents in day-to-day work (typical titles: "How to ...", "How I ...", "How we ...").
3. Reports or comments on a new release from a major frontier lab, including credible user feedback on such a release. Major labs are Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google (Gemini), Meta, and leading Chinese teams: DeepSeek, Alibaba (Qwen), Moonshot AI (Kimi), Zhipu (GLM), ByteDance (Doubao / Seed), MiniMax, and Tencent (Hunyuan).

Do not use this profile for releases or benchmarks of small, niche, or fine-tuned models from other labs, funding, valuations, company strategy or business news, personnel news, policy and regulation, event or podcast promotion, general commentary on AI's effect on society or jobs, or link roundups without substantial original content.
