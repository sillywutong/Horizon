# Economics essays profile

Use this profile for essays, commentary, and explanatory writing about economics from a few trusted authors (such as Cory Doctorow, Brian Potter of Construction Physics, and Martin Alderson): opinion pieces and reviews on markets, platforms, industries, technology, and policy, and explanations of economic models, mechanisms, and cost structures.

This profile is assigned explicitly to selected sources, and every item from them is kept. Do not use it for market news, earnings, or price movements from other sources.
