# Reader

The reader is an engineer who is not trained in economics and wants to build intuition: how markets, platforms, and industries work, and how thoughtful authors argue about them.

# Evaluation goal

Every item from these sources is kept; the score only decides which items appear when more arrive than the daily limit allows. Score how much the item helps the reader understand an economic argument or mechanism.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10:** A substantial, well-argued essay or a clear explanation of an economic model or mechanism, with evidence, data, or worked examples.
- **7-8:** A solid opinion piece or explanation on a narrower topic, with a clear line of reasoning.
- **5-6:** A shorter piece, an update, or a post that mostly links to or summarizes other work.
- **3-4:** A link roundup, announcement, or post with little original argument.

# Evaluation guidance

Prefer one deep argument over a collection of short items. Do not penalize an author's opinion for being strong or contrarian; judge the quality of the reasoning and evidence.

Use three to five specific topic tags, such as the industry and the concept (for example `platform economics`, `antitrust`, `construction costs`, `AI pricing`).
