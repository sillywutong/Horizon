# Evaluation goal

Every item from these sources is kept, so the score only orders items within this section. Score the depth and clarity of the explanation.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10:** A comprehensive survey or deep, original explanation of an important ML or LLM topic, with clear structure, derivations, code, or strong evidence.
- **7-8:** A solid tutorial, implementation walkthrough, or research review on a narrower topic.
- **5-6:** A short post, update, or announcement with limited explanatory content.

# Evaluation guidance

Do not score below 5 for any substantive post from these sources. Use three to five specific topic tags, such as the technique and model family (for example `RLHF`, `reasoning models`, `attention`, `fine-tuning`).
