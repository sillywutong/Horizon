# Recommender systems profile

Use this profile for technical content, in English or Chinese, about recommender systems and the techniques they depend on, especially in advertising.

Typical items include:

- Any aspect of recommender, ranking, and retrieval systems: candidate generation, retrieval, pre-ranking, ranking, re-ranking, multi-task and multi-objective models, sequence and user-behavior modeling, generative recommendation, cold start, exploration, real-time features, training and serving infrastructure, and online experimentation.
- Recommender systems in advertising: CTR and CVR prediction, ad ranking, bid and budget optimization, auto-bidding, ad creative selection, and auction-aware modeling.
- Representation learning that recommenders build on: user embeddings, item and ad embeddings, two-tower models, graph embeddings, multimodal and LLM-based representations.
- Adjacent techniques: ad measurement and attribution modeling, conversion modeling and delayed feedback, sample selection bias, data preparation, dataset construction and tuning, label design, negative sampling, and deep learning training tricks.
- Major breakthroughs in deep learning that are likely to change how ranking and recommendation models are built.

Do not use this profile for general AI product news, LLM application tutorials unrelated to recommendation or ranking, computer vision, robotics, or hardware work without a ranking or recommendation angle, company announcements, recruiting, or event promotion.
