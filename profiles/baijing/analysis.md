# Reader

The reader is an engineer at a programmatic advertising platform who has never run marketing from the advertiser side. They read Chinese outbound-marketing (出海) content only to learn how real companies acquire users and grow overseas, and why those efforts succeed or fail. They have very limited time for this source, so be strict: most items from these feeds should be filtered out.

# Evaluation goal

Score whether the item teaches a concrete, transferable lesson about marketing or growth through a specific case or a clear mechanism. Most items are written in Chinese; judge them by the same standard as English content. Look at both the title and the body.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Essential.** An outstanding case study or analysis: the title contains 有一套, 案例, 以…为例, or 故事, or the article is a how-to analysis of achieving a specific marketing goal (如何做到…, 怎么做到…), or it explains the mechanism behind an outcome (titles with 为什么, 为何). It names the company or product, describes the strategy, channels, and tactics in detail, and gives concrete data or results.
- **7-8: High value.** An item matching one of the patterns above, with real specifics but thinner data, a narrower scope, or less depth. Items whose title contains 有一套, 案例, 以…为例, or 故事 should score at least 7 unless the body turns out to be an advertisement, course promotion, or event promotion.
- **6: Growth story.** The growth story of a specific company or business, with some explanation of how it grew, but without a clear marketing method or mechanism.
- **5: Failure story.** The failure, decline, or retreat story of a specific company or business, with some explanation of what went wrong. Score failure stories below otherwise comparable growth stories.
- **3-4: Low value.** Interviews, opinion pieces, or market overviews that mention companies but do not build a concrete case or explain a mechanism.
- **0-2: Noise.** Industry news, revenue or download rankings, funding and investment, company announcements, personnel changes, product launches without marketing detail, event or conference promotion, recruiting, advertorials, and anything not about marketing or growth.

# Evaluation guidance

Title keywords are a strong signal, not a guarantee: confirm with the body that the article actually delivers a case, a method, or a mechanism. Do not reward length, hype, or large headline numbers by themselves. Distinguish company claims and vendor marketing from measured results.

Use three to five specific topic tags, such as the company or product name, the market, and the tactic (for example `TikTok投放`, `东南亚`, `KOL营销`, `短剧出海`).
