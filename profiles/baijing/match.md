# China outbound marketing profile (strict)

Use this profile for Chinese-language content about Chinese companies going overseas (出海), from sources such as 白鲸出海 and 白鲸社区, when the item explains how a specific company or product acquired users, grew, or failed in overseas markets, or explains the mechanism behind a marketing or growth outcome.

Typical items include marketing and growth case studies, how-to analyses of achieving a specific marketing goal, explanations of why something worked or failed, and growth or failure stories of a specific company or business.

Do not use this profile for industry news, revenue or download rankings, funding and investment, company announcements, personnel changes, event or conference promotion, policy news, or general market overviews that are not built around a concrete case or mechanism.
